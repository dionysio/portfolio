Experience

July 2019 - present

Bringg (formerly Zenkraft) - Multi-Carrier Shipping Platform

Zenkraft provided shipping, tracking and returns directly in Salesforce, used by over 50,000 users. It was acquired by Bringg, a delivery management platform, in 2022. I joined as a contract Python engineer in 2019 to migrate the platform to Heroku/PostgreSQL, took ownership of the backend, and grew into Engineering Team Lead and, since April 2025, Director of Technology.

Carrier platform. Carrier coverage grew from about 25 to 221 integrations; I built 51 myself and scoped most of the rest. I wrote the framework that cut new integrations from weeks to days, and led the carrier API migrations forced by sunsets (UPS and FedEx SOAP to REST, USPS, Royal Mail, DHL Germany, Yodel to InPost) and regulatory changes (UK Windsor Framework, EU de minimis, US CPSC eFiling).

Heroku to GCP/Kubernetes. Led the migration: hosting cost fell by about half while shipment volume grew about 7x. I built observability from scratch (metrics, alerting, post-release checks) and used it to fix request hangs, 502s, pod crashes and DB deadlocks, taking production incidents from roughly monthly to about two a year.

Peak season. After a hard 2024 Black Friday, I replaced firefighting with capacity planning, autoscaling, batch shipment preparation and live metrics. The 2025 peak went smoothly.

Leadership. Grew the organisation from one team to three (about 11 people) and back to one. I now lead 4 senior engineers and 1 QA, all hired and mentored by me, including an engineer later promoted to Tech Lead. I report to executive leadership and am the main technical contact for carriers and customers.

AI enablement. Built Claude Code agents and skills for my team and others, including a self-service production data assistant that customer success and product use to answer customer questions without engineering help.
